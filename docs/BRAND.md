# Brand

- **Name:** Slotwell (always one word, capital S; the wordmark is lowercase `slotwell`)
- **Tagline:** Book a time. No back-and-forth.
- **Alternates:** Your calendar, open for business. · Scheduling that feels like your site.
- **One-liner:** A fast, minimal scheduler for freelancers: share a link, let clients pick a free slot, and get a confirmed meeting in your calendar.
- **Short description:** Slotwell is a self-hosted, single-owner scheduling tool. It checks your Google Calendar, shows clients only your real free times, and books meetings with a Meet link and email confirmations — no accounts needed for guests.
- **Logo:** rounded square outline in accent purple, one horizontal slot in the foreground colour, a purple "booked" dot on the right (`src/components/brand/logo.tsx`, `src/app/icon.svg`).
- **Colours:** background `#0b0b0c` / `#fafafa`, foreground `#f5f5f5` / `#0a0a0a`, accent `#a100ff` (shared with prashanta.dev).
- **Type:** Geist Sans, Geist Mono.
- **Voice:** short, friendly, no jargon — "Pick a time", "You're booked", "Need a different time?"
